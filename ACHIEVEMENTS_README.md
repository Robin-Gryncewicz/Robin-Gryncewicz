## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I earn achievements in private repositories?**
A: Some achievements (like Pull Shark) can be earned in private repos, but they won't be publicly visible unless you enable private contributions in your profile settings.

**Q: How long does it take for achievements to appear?**
A: Usually within minutes, but can take up to 24 hours in some cases.

**Q: Can achievements be lost?**
A: No, once earned, achievements are permanent.

**Q: Are there achievements I can no longer earn?**
A: Yes, historical achievements like "Arctic Code Vault Contributor" and "Mars 2020 Contributor" were time-limited.

**Q: Do achievements affect my GitHub ranking or profile visibility?**
A: They don't directly affect algorithmic ranking, but they can make your profile more impressive to visitors and potential employers.
